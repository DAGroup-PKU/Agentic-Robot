pyrualean-math.woff sets the equations of the PyRUA-Lean post (content/articles/fewer-tokens-better-action.template.html).

It is a subset of Latin Modern Math 1.959 (latinmodern-math.otf, TeX Live), made with
scripts/build_math_font.py and renamed "PyRUA-Lean Math", as the license requests for derived fonts.
